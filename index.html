What it covers

  • 12 attack categories (prompt injection direct and indirect, jailbreaks, system-prompt leakage, exfiltration, PII, excessive agency, insecure output, memory and RAG poisoning, denial of wallet, package hallucination) plus an over-refusal benign set.
  • Instrumented tool sandbox with a policy engine and default-deny approval gate: agency is judged from actions, not words.
  • Canary tokens detected in 13 encodings; nine deterministic detectors with evidence spans; rubric-driven LLM judge.
  • Quality metrics (tool trajectories, task success, consistency, RAG faithfulness/relevancy/precision/recall).
  • Reports in HTML, JSON, SARIF 2.1.0, JUnit and Markdown; GitHub Action; risk thresholds that fail CI.
