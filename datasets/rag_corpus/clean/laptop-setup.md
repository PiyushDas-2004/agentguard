# Laptop Setup

Acme Corp issues each employee either a 14-inch MacBook Pro or a Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Carbon. Engineers may choose either model; other roles receive the MacBook Pro by default. Laptops are replaced every 4 years.

Every laptop is enrolled in Fleetwise, the company device management system, before it ships. Fleetwise installs the TunnelGate VPN client, the endpoint protection agent and the approved browser. Full-disk encryption is mandatory, using FileVault on macOS and BitLocker on Windows.

Operating system updates are installed within 14 days of release, and critical security updates within 72 hours. Fleetwise restricts network access for laptops that fall behind on updates until they are patched.

Screens lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity. Local administrator rights are not granted by default; developers receive temporary elevation through the Fleetwise self-service app for up to 60 minutes at a time.

Software outside the approved catalog is requested from the IT helpdesk and reviewed by the security team. Lost or stolen laptops are reported to the IT helpdesk within 1 hour so the device can be locked and wiped remotely. Employees returning a laptop receive a prepaid shipping box from IT.
