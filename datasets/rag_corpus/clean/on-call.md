# On-Call

Acme Corp engineering teams share production on-call duty in a weekly rotation. Each rotation starts on Monday at 10:00 am Eastern and lasts seven days. Every rotation has a primary and a secondary engineer. The on-call rota is maintained in PagerLine, the paging system, and each team lead updates it at least four weeks ahead.

The primary on-call engineer acknowledges a page within 5 minutes. If the primary does not acknowledge, PagerLine escalates to the secondary after 10 minutes and to the engineering manager after 20 minutes.

On-call engineers keep a laptop and a working internet connection within reach and stay able to respond within 15 minutes during their shift. Engineers receive an on-call stipend of $300 per week. A night with more than two pages after midnight earns a recovery morning off the next day.

The outgoing engineer writes handoff notes covering open incidents, noisy alerts and pending follow-ups, and reviews them with the incoming engineer at the Monday handoff.

Shift swaps are arranged between engineers and recorded in PagerLine before the shift starts. New engineers shadow two full rotations before joining the rota as primary.
