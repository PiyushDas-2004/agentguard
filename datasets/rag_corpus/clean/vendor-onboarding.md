# Vendor Onboarding

Acme Corp onboards every new supplier before the first purchase order is issued. The onboarding process is managed by the procurement team in BuyRight and usually takes 10 business days.

Each vendor provides a completed tax form, such as a W-9 for US vendors or a W-8BEN-E for foreign vendors, along with bank account details on company letterhead. Bank details are verified by a call-back to a phone number from an independent source before they are saved, which protects against payment fraud.

Vendors that will process or store Acme customer data complete a security questionnaire and provide a current SOC 2 Type II report or ISO 27001 certificate. The security team reviews these documents within 5 business days and assigns a risk rating of low, medium or high. High-risk vendors are reviewed again every year.

Vendors also sign the Acme supplier code of conduct and, when needed, a data processing agreement. Once approval is complete, BuyRight assigns a vendor number, and invoices from that vendor can be matched to purchase orders.

Changes to a vendor's bank details follow the same call-back verification as the original setup. Vendors with no activity for 18 months are deactivated automatically.
