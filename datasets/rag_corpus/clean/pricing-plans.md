# Pricing Plans

Acme Cloud is sold in four subscription plans. Prices are per workspace and billed in US dollars.

The Starter plan costs $29 per month and includes up to 5 users, 50 GB of storage and community support. The Team plan costs $99 per month and includes up to 25 users, 500 GB of storage and business-hours e-mail support. The Business plan costs $299 per month and includes up to 100 users, 2 TB of storage, single sign-on and priority support. The Enterprise plan has custom pricing, unlimited users, a dedicated account manager and 24/7 support.

Annual billing gives two months free, which equals a discount of about 17% compared with monthly billing. Additional users beyond a plan's limit cost $6 per user per month on Team and $5 per user per month on Business.

Every new workspace starts with a 14-day free trial of the Business plan, and no payment card is needed for the trial. At the end of the trial, the workspace moves to the Starter plan unless a paid plan is selected.

Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated. Downgrades take effect at the start of the next billing period. Nonprofit organizations and schools receive a 30% discount on Team and Business plans.
