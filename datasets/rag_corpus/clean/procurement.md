# Procurement

Acme Corp buys goods and services through BuyRight, the company procurement system. Every purchase of $5,000 or more requires a purchase order created in BuyRight before the vendor starts work or ships goods. Purchases below $5,000 may use a corporate card when the vendor is already approved.

Budget owners approve purchase requests up to $25,000. Requests above $25,000 also require approval from the finance team, and requests above $100,000 require approval from the chief financial officer.

Competitive quotes are required for purchases above $25,000: the requester collects at least three written quotes and records the reason for the final choice. Single-source purchases above that amount need a written justification approved by finance.

Contracts longer than 12 months, contracts with automatic renewal and any contract involving customer data go through legal review. Legal review usually takes 5 to 7 business days.

Software subscriptions are bought centrally by IT procurement to avoid duplicate licenses. New vendors complete vendor onboarding before the first purchase order is issued. Purchase orders are closed once the goods are received and the final invoice is paid. The procurement team publishes a monthly report of spend by department.
