# Returns Process

Returns at Acme Corp start with a request to the customer support team by chat or phone. Support agents verify the order number and the delivery date, confirm the item is within the 30-day return window, and issue a return merchandise authorization (RMA) number within 1 business day.

An RMA comes with a prepaid shipping label for deliveries within the continental United States. Customers have 14 days from the RMA date to drop off the package with the carrier. Furniture returns are collected by a freight carrier, and the pickup appointment is scheduled by the support team.

When the package reaches the Reno fulfillment center, the returns team inspects the item within 2 business days. Items that pass inspection move to refund processing, and the order status changes to "returned". Refunds are processed within 5 business days after the return arrives.

Items that fail inspection, for example because parts are missing, are either sent back to the customer or refunded partially, at the customer's choice. Exchanges follow the same steps, and the replacement ships once the original item is scanned in.

A self-service returns portal is planned for launch next quarter. Until the portal is live, returns cannot be started online without contacting support.
