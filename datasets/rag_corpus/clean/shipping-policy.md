# Shipping Policy

Acme Corp ships orders from its fulfillment center in Reno, Nevada. Orders placed before 2:00 pm Eastern time on a business day leave the warehouse the same day; later orders ship the next business day.

Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days within the continental United States and is free for orders over $100. Orders under $100 pay a flat $8.95 standard shipping fee. Expedited shipping costs $19 and arrives in 2 business days. Overnight shipping costs $39 and is available for orders placed before noon Eastern time.

Furniture such as standing desks ships by freight carrier and requires a delivery appointment. Freight deliveries usually arrive within 7 business days, and the carrier calls the customer one day ahead to confirm a delivery window.

International shipping is available to 28 countries and takes 7 to 14 business days. International customers pay import duties and taxes on delivery. Acme does not ship to post office boxes for freight items.

Every order receives a tracking number by e-mail once it ships. An order shows the status "shipped" when it leaves the warehouse and "delivered" once the carrier confirms delivery. Lost packages are reshipped at no cost after a 5 business day carrier trace.
