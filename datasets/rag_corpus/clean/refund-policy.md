# Refund Policy

Acme Corp offers refunds on hardware purchased directly from the Acme online store. Customers may request a refund within 30 days of the delivery date. Refunds are processed within 5 business days after the returned item arrives at the Acme fulfillment center in Reno, Nevada.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method. Card refunds typically appear on the customer's statement two to three days after Acme processes them, depending on the issuing bank. Orders paid with Acme store credit are refunded as store credit.

Items must be returned in their original packaging with all accessories. Opened electronics, such as the USB-C dock, the 4K monitor and the noise-cancelling headset, carry a 15% restocking fee. Unopened items and furniture, including standing desks and ergonomic chairs, carry no restocking fee.

Original shipping charges are not refundable unless the item arrived damaged or Acme shipped the wrong product. Customers who choose store credit instead of a refund receive a 10% bonus on the credit amount. Refunds for orders older than 30 days are handled as warranty claims instead. Partial refunds apply when a returned item is missing parts or shows wear beyond normal inspection.
