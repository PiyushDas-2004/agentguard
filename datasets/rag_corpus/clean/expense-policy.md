# Expense Policy

Acme Corp reimburses reasonable business expenses that employees pay personally. Expense reports are submitted in Ledgerly, the company expense system, within 30 days of the purchase date. Reports submitted after 60 days are not reimbursed without approval from the finance director.

Itemized receipts are required for every expense of $25 or more. Managers approve expense reports up to $1,000; reports above $1,000 also require director approval. Approved expenses are reimbursed by direct deposit in the next bi-weekly payroll run.

The daily meal allowance is $75 for domestic travel and $90 for international travel. Client entertainment is limited to $150 per attendee and requires the names of all attendees in the report. Alcohol is reimbursed only as part of client entertainment.

Home office equipment is covered by the remote work stipend rather than the expense policy. Software subscriptions are bought through procurement and are not reimbursed as personal expenses. Mileage for personal vehicles is reimbursed at $0.67 per mile.

Corporate cards are issued to employees who travel more than four times a year. Corporate card transactions are reconciled in Ledgerly by the 5th of the following month. Expenses that break this policy are returned to the employee for correction.
