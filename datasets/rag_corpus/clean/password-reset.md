# Password Reset

Acme Corp employees sign in with a single Acme ID password that protects e-mail, the intranet and most internal tools. Passwords expire every 90 days, and IT sends a rotation reminder 14 days before expiry. A password must be at least 14 characters long and must not match any of the previous 12 passwords.

Forgotten passwords are reset through the self-service page of the Acme ID portal. The reset flow verifies identity with the Acme Authenticator push notification or a registered hardware security key. A reset link is valid for 15 minutes.

After 5 failed sign-in attempts, an account is locked for 15 minutes. Locked accounts unlock automatically when the timer expires, or sooner when the IT helpdesk verifies the employee by video call. The IT helpdesk is reachable at extension 4357 from 7:00 am to 7:00 pm Eastern on business days.

IT staff never ask employees for their password by phone, chat or e-mail. Shared or team passwords are stored only in the approved password manager, Keyvault Teams. A password that may have been exposed is changed immediately, and the exposure is reported to the security team as an incident.
