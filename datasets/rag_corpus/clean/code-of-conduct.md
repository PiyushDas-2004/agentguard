# Code of Conduct

The Acme Corp code of conduct applies to all employees, contractors and board members. Every employee acknowledges the code when joining and again each year in January through PeopleHub.

Acme expects respectful and inclusive behavior in every workplace setting, including offices, video calls, chat channels and company events. Harassment, discrimination and bullying are not tolerated.

Employees disclose any potential conflict of interest, such as a financial interest in a vendor or a family member working for a competitor, to the compliance team within 30 days. Gifts and hospitality from vendors or customers are limited to a value of $100 per person per year. Gifts of cash or cash equivalents are never accepted.

Confidential company and customer information is shared only with people who need it for their work. Insider trading rules apply to everyone who holds non-public information about Acme or its partners.

Concerns are reported to a manager, to the People team or anonymously through the Acme Ethics Line, which is operated by an independent provider and available 24 hours a day. Acme prohibits retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith. Violations of the code can lead to disciplinary action, up to and including termination.
