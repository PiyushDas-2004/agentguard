# Travel Policy

Business travel at Acme Corp is booked through TripDesk, the company travel portal. Flights and hotels are booked at least 14 days before departure whenever possible; bookings made later need a short justification for the approver.

Economy class is standard for flights shorter than 6 hours. Premium economy is allowed for flights of 6 hours or longer, and business class requires approval from a vice president. Domestic trips are approved by the traveler's manager; international trips also need vice president approval.

Hotel stays are capped at $250 per night. In high-cost cities, namely New York, San Francisco, London and Zurich, the cap rises to $325 per night. Travelers choose hotels from the TripDesk preferred list when one is available within 3 miles of the destination.

Rental cars are limited to the intermediate class, and ride-share or taxi trips are preferred for short distances. Rail is the default for trips under 4 hours in Europe and the US Northeast corridor.

Travel insurance and 24/7 emergency assistance through the TripDesk assistance line cover every booked trip. Meals during travel follow the daily allowance in the expense policy. Unused nonrefundable tickets are recorded in TripDesk as credits for future trips.
