# Service Level Agreement

The Acme Cloud service level agreement (SLA) defines the monthly uptime commitment for paid plans. Uptime is measured each calendar month by external probes that test the API and the web app every minute.

The Team and Business plans carry a 99.9% monthly uptime commitment. The Enterprise plan carries a 99.95% monthly uptime commitment. The Starter plan and free trials have no uptime commitment.

When monthly uptime falls below the commitment, the customer receives a service credit. Uptime below 99.9% earns a credit of 10% of that month's subscription fee. Uptime below 99.0% earns a 25% credit, and uptime below 95.0% earns a 50% credit. Service credits are applied to the next invoice and are never paid out in cash.

Customers request service credits within 30 days after the end of the affected month by opening a support ticket. Scheduled maintenance announced at least 48 hours in advance does not count as downtime. Maintenance windows take place on Sundays between 02:00 and 04:00 UTC.

Downtime caused by customer actions, third-party internet failures or force majeure events is excluded. The SLA is the customer's sole remedy for unavailability.
