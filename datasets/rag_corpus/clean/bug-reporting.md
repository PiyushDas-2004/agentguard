# Bug Reporting

Bugs in Acme products are tracked in Trackwell, the company issue tracker. A good bug report has a short title, the product version, steps to reproduce, the expected result, the actual result and screenshots or logs when available.

Each bug receives a priority from P0 to P3. P0 is a critical bug that blocks many customers or causes data loss, and it is triaged within 2 hours, at any time of day. P1 is a major bug without a workaround and is triaged within 1 business day. P2 is a bug with a workaround and P3 is a minor or cosmetic issue; both are triaged in the weekly bug triage meeting on Wednesdays.

The triage team assigns each accepted bug to an owning team and a target release. P0 fixes ship as hotfixes outside the regular release train. P1 fixes ship in the next release train.

Bugs reported by customers keep a link to the original support ticket so the support team can update the customer when the fix ships. Duplicate bugs are closed and linked to the original report.

Security vulnerabilities are not filed as regular bugs. They go to the security team through the private vulnerability form in Trackwell so that details stay restricted.
