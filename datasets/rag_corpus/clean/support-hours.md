# Support Hours

Acme Corp customer support is staffed by teams in Austin, Dublin and Bengaluru. Standard support hours are Monday to Friday, 8:00 am to 8:00 pm Eastern time.

Coverage depends on the subscription plan. Starter customers receive community forum support and e-mail support during standard hours. Team customers receive e-mail and chat support during standard hours. Business customers receive 24/5 support from Sunday 8:00 pm to Friday 8:00 pm Eastern. Enterprise customers receive 24/7 support, including phone support, every day of the year.

First response targets are 2 business days for Starter, 1 business day for Team, 4 hours for Business and 1 hour for urgent Enterprise tickets. Gold-tier customers are placed at the front of the queue on every channel.

Support is closed on Acme company holidays for Starter and Team customers. Business and Enterprise customers keep their normal coverage on holidays.

Customers reach support through the help center chat, by e-mail or, for Enterprise only, by phone. The help center also offers the knowledge base and community forum around the clock. Status of ongoing outages is published on the Acme status page rather than through support tickets.
