# Office Locations

Acme Corp has four offices. Headquarters is in Austin, Texas, at 412 Congress Avenue, and hosts the executive team, finance and most of product engineering. The Austin office has 380 desks across three floors.

The Toronto office at 88 Front Street West hosts the design team and part of customer support for the Americas. The Dublin office on Grand Canal Quay is the hub for Europe, the Middle East and Africa, with sales, support and the security operations team. The Bengaluru office in Koramangala hosts engineering and Asia-Pacific support.

All offices are open from 6:00 am to 10:00 pm local time on business days, and badge access is required at every entrance. Weekend access is available to employees with approval from the workplace team.

Desks in every office are booked through the Deskly app up to 14 days in advance. Each office has bookable meeting rooms, a wellness room and bike storage.

Visitors are registered by their host at least 24 hours before arrival and receive a temporary badge at reception. Every office has a first-aid kit and trained first-aid volunteers on each floor.
