# Holiday Calendar

Acme Corp observes 11 paid company holidays per year in the United States. The US holidays are New Year's Day, Martin Luther King Jr. Day, Memorial Day, Juneteenth, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day, the day after Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve and Christmas Day, plus one floating holiday.

The floating holiday is chosen by each employee and recorded in PeopleHub at least 2 weeks in advance. An unused floating holiday does not carry over into the next year.

When a holiday falls on a Saturday, it is observed on the preceding Friday. When a holiday falls on a Sunday, it is observed on the following Monday.

Offices in Toronto, Dublin and Bengaluru follow local public holidays instead of the US list, and each office publishes its own calendar in December for the coming year. Each office also observes at least 10 paid holidays.

Employees who are on call or staff Enterprise support during a company holiday receive a replacement day off, which is used within 60 days. Company holidays do not count against PTO. Customer support for Starter and Team plans is closed on company holidays.
