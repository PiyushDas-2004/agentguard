# Warranty

Acme Corp products carry a limited warranty against defects in materials and workmanship. The warranty period starts on the delivery date and depends on the product line.

Standing desks have a 5-year warranty on the frame and lift motors and a 2-year warranty on the desktop surface. Ergonomic chairs have a 3-year warranty covering the base, gas cylinder and mechanism; upholstery is covered for 1 year. The 4K monitor has a 2-year warranty that includes a zero dead pixel guarantee during the first 30 days. The USB-C dock and the noise-cancelling headset have a 1-year warranty.

Warranty claims are filed through the Acme Care portal with the order number and a photo or short video of the defect. Acme Care reviews each claim within 2 business days and issues a return merchandise authorization (RMA) number when a replacement is approved. Approved replacements ship within 10 business days.

The warranty does not cover accidental damage, cosmetic wear, damage from unauthorized repairs, or products bought from unauthorized resellers. Replacement parts are covered for the remainder of the original warranty period or 90 days, whichever is longer. Extended warranty plans, sold as Acme Care Plus, add 2 years of coverage for $49 per product.
