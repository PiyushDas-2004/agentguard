# VPN Access

Acme Corp employees reach internal systems from outside the office through the TunnelGate VPN client. TunnelGate is installed automatically on every managed laptop by Fleetwise device management. Personal devices cannot connect to the VPN.

TunnelGate authenticates with Acme ID single sign-on and the Acme Authenticator second factor. The client connects to the nearest of three gateways: Austin for the Americas, Dublin for Europe and Africa, and Bengaluru for Asia-Pacific. Split tunneling is disabled, so all traffic flows through the corporate network while the VPN is connected.

VPN sessions disconnect after 30 minutes of inactivity and end after a maximum of 10 hours. The VPN is not required for Acme ID, e-mail or the intranet home page, which are reachable from the internet with single sign-on.

Contractors receive VPN access only with written approval from their Acme manager, and contractor access expires after 90 days unless renewed. Access from countries under trade sanctions is blocked.

Connection problems are usually solved by reconnecting to a different gateway or restarting the TunnelGate client. Persistent issues are handled by the IT helpdesk at extension 4357, and VPN outages are announced on the Acme status page.
