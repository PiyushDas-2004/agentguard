# Customer Escalation

Acme Corp support uses a three-tier escalation path. Tier 1 agents handle incoming tickets and resolve common questions about orders, billing and accounts. When a ticket needs deeper product knowledge, Tier 1 escalates it to Tier 2 specialists, who respond within 4 business hours.

Tier 2 escalates confirmed product defects to the engineering team by filing a bug in Trackwell with the ticket link. Engineering acknowledges escalated bugs within 1 business day for paying customers.

A customer may ask for an escalation at any time. Escalation requests are reviewed by the duty escalation manager, who contacts the customer within 1 business day with an owner and an action plan.

Gold-tier customers are routed to a named support lead, and their escalations skip directly to Tier 2. Silver-tier customers receive priority routing during peak periods. Bronze-tier customers follow the standard queue.

Executive escalations for Enterprise customers involve the account manager and the vice president of customer success. These escalations receive a status update every business day until the issue is resolved.

Escalated tickets stay open until the customer confirms the resolution or 7 days pass without a reply. Every escalated ticket is reviewed in the weekly support quality meeting.
