# Remote Work

Acme Corp follows a hybrid work model. Employees assigned to an office work on site at least two days a week, and Tuesday and Thursday are the shared in-office days for most teams. Fully remote roles are approved by a vice president and noted in PeopleHub.

New remote and hybrid employees receive a one-time home office stipend of $500 for a desk, chair or monitor. Acme also pays a monthly internet allowance of $50, which is added to payroll automatically.

Core collaboration hours are 10:00 am to 3:00 pm in the employee's local time zone. Meetings across regions are scheduled inside the overlapping core hours whenever possible.

Employees may work from another country for up to 4 weeks per calendar year with manager approval and a tax review by the People team. Work from countries under trade sanctions is not permitted.

Remote work uses managed laptops only. Company data is accessed through the TunnelGate VPN when internal systems require it, and video calls in public places use headphones and a privacy screen. Equipment bought with the home office stipend belongs to the employee, while laptops and monitors issued by IT are returned when employment ends.
