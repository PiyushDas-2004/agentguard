# PTO Policy

Full-time employees at Acme Corp receive 20 days of paid time off (PTO) per calendar year. PTO accrues monthly at 1.67 days per month, starting on the first day of employment. Part-time employees accrue PTO in proportion to their scheduled hours.

Up to 5 unused PTO days carry over into the next calendar year. Carried-over days expire on March 31 if they are not used. Unused PTO is paid out at termination where local law requires it.

PTO requests are entered in PeopleHub and approved by the employee's manager. Requests for more than 3 consecutive days are submitted at least 2 weeks in advance. Requests during the year-end freeze in the last two weeks of December need director approval for engineering and support teams.

Sick leave is separate from PTO. Employees receive 10 paid sick days per year, and sick days do not require advance notice. A doctor's note is needed only for absences longer than 5 consecutive working days.

Parental leave provides 16 weeks of fully paid leave for all new parents. Bereavement leave provides up to 5 paid days. Company holidays are listed in the holiday calendar and do not count against PTO.
