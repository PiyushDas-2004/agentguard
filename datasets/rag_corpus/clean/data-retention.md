# Data Retention

Acme Corp keeps data only as long as it is needed for business, legal or contractual reasons. The data governance team reviews this schedule every year in January.

Financial records, including invoices, payment records and tax documents, are retained for 7 years. Customer support tickets are retained for 3 years after the ticket is closed. Application logs are retained for 90 days, and security audit logs for 1 year. Database backups are retained for 35 days.

Customer account data is kept while the account is active and deleted within 30 days after the account is closed, except where financial retention rules apply. Customer deletion requests under privacy laws are completed within 30 days, and the customer receives a confirmation when deletion is finished.

Employee records are retained for 6 years after employment ends. Recruiting records for candidates who were not hired are deleted after 2 years.

Data is deleted automatically by scheduled jobs in each system. Legal holds pause deletion for any data related to litigation or an investigation; the legal team places and releases holds. Exceptions to this schedule require written approval from the data governance team and the legal team.
